## Goal

Complete the two financial branches intentionally deferred by 11.5.4:

```text
1. A negative DATE_CHANGE, including an authorized reduction in the number of
   confirmed nights, applies the requested dates and pricing atomically and
   creates one exact lifecycle-adjustment Refund against the validated
   INITIAL_RESERVATION Payment.
2. An APPROVED LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Payment whose final date mutation cannot
   commit preserves the original Reservation and creates one exact compensating
   lifecycle-adjustment Refund against that approved adjustment Payment.
```

Refund execution and evidence reconciliation reuse the accepted Phase 11.4
Tilopay type-2 workflow. Refund failure never restores old dates after a
successful negative completion and never mutates the original dates during a
failed positive completion.

## Persistence Change

`RefundAuthorizationType` gains:

```text
LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT
```

The migration only extends the existing PostgreSQL enum. No table, column,
index, dependency, environment variable, or seed change is introduced.

Lifecycle-adjustment Refund rows remain normal auditable `Refund` records:

```text
paymentId = exact payment being returned
lifecycleRequestId = owning DATE_CHANGE or STAY_EXTENSION request
authorizationType = LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT
amount = exact negative difference or exact approved adjustment payment
status = PENDING
processingMode = TILOPAY_API when the payment has a provider order reference,
                 otherwise TILOPAY_PORTAL_FALLBACK
```

## Negative-Difference Completion

A negative approval is no longer left in `APPROVED` awaiting a future phase.
The protected admin decision route dispatches negative approvals to a dedicated
Serializable transaction.

Required state:

```text
- Request is a DATE_CHANGE with financialDifference < 0. A shortened stay is
  represented as DATE_CHANGE even when the original check-in is preserved.
- STAY_EXTENSION remains the specialized positive branch that preserves the
  original check-in and moves check-out to a later date.
- Request is PENDING_REVIEW, or APPROVED from an accepted pre-11.5.5 replay.
- financialDifference < 0.
- Review window, request version, and Reservation.updatedAt remain valid.
- Reservation and property still pass the final 11.5.4 snapshot and timing rules.
- Requested stay and preparation buffers remain available.
- The validated INITIAL_RESERVATION Payment remains linked and currency aligned.
- Remaining captured balance covers abs(financialDifference), including every
  committed standard, extraordinary, and lifecycle-adjustment Refund.
```

One transaction performs:

```text
PENDING_REVIEW -> APPROVED
final snapshot/timing/availability validation
create exact PENDING LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Refund
apply requested Reservation dates and pricing
APPROVED -> COMPLETED
supersede old arrival-instruction intents
create one new eligible arrival intent
write bounded approval, Refund authorization, and completion audit records
```

Any failure rolls back approval, Refund creation, Reservation mutation, arrival
supersession, and completion together.

The successful Reservation retains:

```text
same Reservation.id
status = CONFIRMED
same confirmedAt
same expiresAt behavior
requested dates and pricing
```

Refund execution may later become APPROVED or FAILED without changing those
completed dates.

## Failed Positive Completion Compensation

Normal provider-result validation remains authoritative. After an exact
`LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT` Payment becomes `APPROVED`, 11.5.4 attempts its final
completion transaction.

For terminal business failures such as stale state, unavailable dates, expired
or mismatched hold, invalid property/reservation state, timing expiry, or an
invalid completion snapshot, 11.5.5 records compensation in a separate
Serializable transaction:

```text
- Reservation remains CONFIRMED with original dates and pricing.
- The APPROVED adjustment Payment is never downgraded or deleted.
- Request becomes FAILED unless it is already EXPIRED.
- ACTIVE hold becomes RELEASED or EXPIRED with a bounded compensation reason.
- One exact PENDING LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Refund is linked to the request and the
  approved adjustment Payment.
- Replay returns the existing Refund and does not duplicate state or audit.
```

Unexpected internal conflicts are not automatically classified as a safe
compensation trigger. They remain operational errors instead of silently
creating a potentially incorrect Refund.

## Refund Execution and Reconciliation

The existing protected routes remain the public administrative boundary:

```text
POST /api/admin/refunds/{refundId}/execute
POST /api/admin/refunds/{refundId}/consult
POST /api/admin/refunds/{refundId}/reconcile
```

They dispatch `LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT` rows to a specialized companion workflow
and keep the accepted standard/extraordinary implementation unchanged.

The lifecycle workflow preserves the Phase 11.4 contract:

```text
- processModification type = 2.
- API execution remains sandbox-only.
- Provider network call occurs after the claim transaction commits.
- HTTP 200 alone is not approval.
- Accepted provider response remains PROCESSING until evidence reconciliation.
- Known rejected responses become FAILED without changing Payment.
- Unknown/timeout outcomes remain PROCESSING and are not blindly retried.
- Consult evidence must match provider reference, order, type, absolute amount,
  currency when present, code, and description.
- Conclusive consult evidence locks outcome, mode, and reference.
- Portal fallback remains explicit and audited.
- Payment becomes PARTIALLY_REFUNDED or REFUNDED only after APPROVED
  reconciliation and cumulative amounts never exceed Payment.amount.
```

## Idempotency and Concurrency

Permanent keys distinguish both automatic lifecycle refund origins:

```text
lifecycle-adjustment/negative-difference/{lifecycleRequestId}
lifecycle-adjustment/compensating-refund/{lifecycleRequestId}/{paymentId}
```

The implementation uses:

```text
- unique clientRequestId and idempotencyKey
- Serializable transactions
- explicit 10-second maxWait and 20-second timeout for lifecycle completion
- bounded P2034 transaction retry
- optimistic request, Reservation, Payment, Refund, and hold fences
- exact relationship validation before execution/reconciliation
```

Concurrent approval or compensation cannot create two lifecycle Refund rows or
two Reservation transitions.

## Admin UI

The reservation detail keeps standard-policy and extraordinary Refunds in the
existing section, filtered to the INITIAL_RESERVATION payment boundary. A
separate lifecycle-adjustment section reuses the same centralized bilingual
refund/date-mutation copy and the accepted execute, consult, and reconcile
controls. This prevents an approved adjustment Payment from appearing as an
extraordinary-refund candidate and avoids raw enum labels, hardcoded visible
text, or native dialogs.

The positive guest adjustment page now gives an authoritative handoff error
precedence over a stale `paymentResult=approved` query parameter. A compensated
or failed completion therefore does not misleadingly claim that requested dates
were applied.
