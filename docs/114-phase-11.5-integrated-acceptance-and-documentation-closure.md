## Final Business Contract

### DATE_CHANGE

```text
- May change both dates or preserve check-in while moving check-out earlier/later.
- Reprices the complete requested stay with current pricing.
- May produce a positive, zero, or negative financial difference.
- A shortened stay is a negative DATE_CHANGE.
```

### STAY_EXTENSION

```text
- Preserves the original check-in.
- Requires requested check-out later than the current check-out.
- Preserves the confirmed original total and prices only added nights.
- Produces a positive difference under the accepted current pricing contract.
```

### Financial branches

```text
Positive difference
-> create one exact LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Payment and one independent 60-minute hold
-> apply requested dates only after approved payment and final revalidation

Zero difference
-> complete atomically without Payment, hold, or Refund

Negative difference
-> apply requested dates/pricing atomically
-> create one exact LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Refund authorization against the initial Payment

Approved positive Payment that cannot complete
-> preserve original dates/pricing
-> create one exact compensating LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Refund against the adjustment Payment
```

