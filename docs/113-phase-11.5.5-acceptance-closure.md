## Accepted Capability

Phase 11.5.5 closes the remaining financial branches of the authorized date-mutation flow.

```text
- A negative DATE_CHANGE applies requested dates and recalculated pricing to the
  existing confirmed Reservation and creates one exact PENDING
  LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Refund against the validated initial Payment.
- A shortened stay is represented as DATE_CHANGE, including when check-in remains
  unchanged and check-out moves earlier.
- STAY_EXTENSION remains the specialized branch that preserves check-in, moves
  check-out later, and prices only added nights; it does not generate a negative
  financial difference under the accepted pricing contract.
- An APPROVED lifecycle-adjustment Payment whose final mutation cannot commit
  preserves the original Reservation dates/pricing and creates one exact
  compensating LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Refund against that adjustment Payment.
- Refund execution, consult evidence, portal fallback, and reconciliation reuse
  the accepted Phase 11.4 provider boundary.
```

## Persistence and Financial State

```text
RefundAuthorizationType includes LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT.
Negative-difference Refund.paymentId points to the INITIAL_RESERVATION Payment.
Compensating Refund.paymentId points to the approved LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Payment.
Every lifecycle Refund remains linked to its ReservationLifecycleRequest.
Refund status remains independent from Reservation lifecycle status.
Payment becomes PARTIALLY_REFUNDED or REFUNDED only after approved reconciliation.
Committed cumulative Refund amounts cannot exceed the captured Payment amount.
```

A failed negative-difference Refund never restores the old dates after the date change completed. A failed compensating Refund never applies the requested dates or downgrades the approved adjustment Payment.

## Atomicity, Idempotency, and Concurrency

The accepted implementation uses Serializable transactions, bounded P2034 retries, optimistic state fences, permanent idempotency keys, and exact relationship validation.

```text
lifecycle-adjustment/negative-difference/{lifecycleRequestId}
lifecycle-adjustment/compensating-refund/{lifecycleRequestId}/{paymentId}
```

Acceptance confirmed:

```text
- Negative approval either commits request approval, Refund authorization,
  Reservation mutation, completion, arrival supersession, and audit together, or
  rolls all of them back.
- Replayed and concurrent negative approvals create one completion and one Refund.
- Replayed and concurrent compensation creates one Refund and one terminal
  request/hold transition.
- Provider execution is never duplicated by database replay behavior.
- Relationship, purpose, amount, currency, Reservation, request, Payment, and hold
  mismatches are rejected before an unsafe provider operation.
```

## Acceptance Clarification

The original implementation matrix described a “negative STAY_EXTENSION.” The accepted domain contract distinguishes the guest's business request from the technical request type:

```text
Reduce a five-night stay to three nights:
- requestType = DATE_CHANGE
- requested check-out may move earlier
- financialDifference may be negative
- exact lifecycle-adjustment Refund is required

Extend a three-night stay to five nights:
- requestType = STAY_EXTENSION
- original check-in is preserved
- requested check-out moves later
- only added nights are priced
- financialDifference is positive under the current pricing rules
```

This clarification required documentation correction only. No implementation change was necessary.
