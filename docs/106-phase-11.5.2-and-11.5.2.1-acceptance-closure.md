## Accepted Backend Behavior

```text
- DATE_CHANGE and STAY_EXTENSION requests are created only by an authenticated admin.
- Confirmed reservation, initial payment, guest/contact, date, pricing, currency, and version snapshots remain immutable on the request.
- Full changes reprice the complete requested stay; extensions preserve the original total and price only added nights.
- Availability excludes only the current reservation and its derived own blockers.
- Every unrelated reservation, pending hold, Airbnb/manual/maintenance block, preparation buffer, composed dependency, and active lifecycle hold remains authoritative.
- Positive, zero, and negative quotes remain PENDING_REVIEW and do not create Payment, Refund, LifecycleRequestHold, Tilopay, email, or Reservation mutations.
- Stale state, replay, concurrent creation, 24-hour expiry, 365-day horizon, check-in/check-out boundaries, and audit history passed.
- Property.checkOutTime is used when configured; end of the Guatemala check-out day is the fallback.
```
