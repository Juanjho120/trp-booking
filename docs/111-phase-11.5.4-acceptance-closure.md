## Accepted Completion Behavior

The complete local/test matrix passed for both successful financial branches:

```text
- Zero-difference DATE_CHANGE completes atomically inside the admin approval transaction.
- Zero-difference STAY_EXTENSION preserves the original check-in and applies the requested checkout and pricing.
- Positive completion runs only after normal server-side Tilopay validation marks the exact LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Payment APPROVED.
- Reservation.id, CONFIRMED status, and confirmedAt are preserved.
- Requested check-in, checkout, and pricing snapshots are applied exactly once.
- Positive completion releases the matching active lifecycle hold with LIFECYCLE_DATE_MUTATION_COMPLETED.
- Zero completion creates no Payment and no LifecycleRequestHold.
```

## Accepted Final Validation and Concurrency

```text
- Completion uses a Serializable transaction with explicit 10-second maxWait and 20-second timeout.
- Prisma P2034 retries the complete idempotent transaction up to three times.
- Immutable Reservation updatedAt, dates, guest count, price components, total, currency, and confirmed state are revalidated.
- DATE_CHANGE and STAY_EXTENSION timing boundaries are revalidated at completion.
- The requested stay and proposed preparation-buffer ranges are revalidated.
- Only the current Reservation and the current request's own hold are excluded.
- Other reservations, direct buffers, Airbnb, manual blocks, maintenance, composed dependencies, and unrelated lifecycle holds remain blocking.
- Redirect replay and concurrent completion produce one Reservation mutation, one COMPLETED transition, and one RELEASED hold.
- Stale snapshots, unavailable dates, invalid or expired holds, and invalid adjustment Payments preserve the original Reservation.
```

## Accepted Arrival and Guest-Page Behavior

```text
- Old PENDING and FAILED ARRIVAL_INSTRUCTIONS tied to the original check-in become SKIPPED.

```
