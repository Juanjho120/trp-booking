## Purpose

Define the authoritative business and engineering contract for authorized date changes and stay extensions before adding mutation endpoints, adjustment checkout, requested-date holds, reservation date updates, or lifecycle-adjustment refunds.

11.5.1 is documentation-only. It adds no application code, Prisma schema change, migration, seed change, dependency, environment variable, visible public/admin copy, provider request, payment attempt, reservation mutation, calendar mutation, email delivery, or PMS behavior.

## Preserved Product Boundary

```text
- Guests cannot directly edit confirmed dates from the public website.
- Requests are received through an approved support channel and recorded by an authorized admin.
- The existing Reservation ID and complete history are preserved.
- Reservation.status remains CONFIRMED after a completed date change or stay extension.
- Payment and Refund own financial adjustments; Reservation owns stay and availability state.
- TRP Booking remains a direct-booking engine. TAMIAS remains the PMS.
- Housekeeping, room assignment, operational task management, and other PMS behavior remain out of scope.
```

## Mandatory Hold Separation

The two hold durations are independent business concepts and must never share one constant or silently change each other.

### Public pending-reservation hold

```text
Purpose: protect dates while a new direct reservation completes initial payment
Duration: 15 minutes
Existing constant: PENDING_RESERVATION_HOLD_DURATION_MINUTES
State owner: Reservation.status = PENDING_PAYMENT plus Reservation.expiresAt
Payment purpose: INITIAL_RESERVATION
```

The existing value remains exactly 15 minutes. Phase 11.5 must not rename it, reuse it, alias it, import it into lifecycle code, or change its behavior.

### Lifecycle-adjustment hold

```text
Purpose: protect requested dates after an admin approves a positive-difference date change or extension while the guest completes the additional payment
Duration: 60 minutes
Future constant: LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT_HOLD_DURATION_MINUTES
State owner: LifecycleRequestHold.status plus LifecycleRequestHold.expiresAt
Request state: AWAITING_ADJUSTMENT_PAYMENT
Payment purpose: LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT
```

Implementation requirements:

```text
- LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT_HOLD_DURATION_MINUTES must be defined in the lifecycle/date-change domain, not in lib/reservations/pending-holds.ts.
- Its value must be 60 and must not derive from PENDING_RESERVATION_HOLD_DURATION_MINUTES.
- LifecycleRequestHold expiration must not update Reservation.expiresAt.
- Public pending-hold expiration must not query or mutate LifecycleRequestHold rows.
- The two expiration paths may share a scheduler invocation only if their queries, state transitions, constants, and audit events remain separate.
- Tests must prove that changing one duration does not affect the other.
```

A lifecycle hold is created only after approval of a request with a positive financial difference. `PENDING_REVIEW` requests do not block requested dates. Zero- and negative-difference requests complete immediately after approval/final validation and therefore do not create a lifecycle hold.

## Initial 11.5 Scope

The initial implementation changes reservation dates only.

```text
Property: unchanged
Guest count: unchanged
Guest identity/contact: unchanged
Reservation source/status: confirmed direct reservation only
Reservation ID: unchanged
Currency: unchanged
```

Although the schema can snapshot `requestedGuestCount`, the initial 11.5 implementation must set it equal to `originalGuestCount`. Guest-count changes require a separately approved pricing and capacity contract and are not bundled into date mutation.

Imported Airbnb events and generic calendar blocks are availability inputs, not mutable reservations under this flow.

## Request Eligibility

Every request must satisfy:

```text
- Reservation exists and status is CONFIRMED.
- Reservation belongs to a supported active property.
- A validated INITIAL_RESERVATION Payment exists and belongs to the reservation.
- Requested dates form a valid [check-in, check-out) range.
- Requested dates differ from the current reservation dates.
- Requested guest count equals the current guest count.
- Requested currency equals the reservation currency.
- The request is recorded by an authenticated authorized admin.
- Channel is EMAIL, PHONE, WHATSAPP, or OTHER.
- One client request UUID maps permanently to one lifecycle request.
```

Only one active date mutation may exist for a reservation across `DATE_CHANGE` and `STAY_EXTENSION`.

Active mutation statuses:

```text
PENDING_REVIEW
APPROVED
AWAITING_ADJUSTMENT_PAYMENT
```

A new date mutation must also be rejected while an active cancellation request exists. Completed, rejected, withdrawn, expired, and failed historical requests remain visible but do not block a new request.

## DATE_CHANGE Contract

A `DATE_CHANGE` moves the complete stay to a requested date range.

```text
- It must be recorded before the original check-in moment in America/Guatemala.
- requestedCheckInDate and requestedCheckOutDate may both change.
- The complete requested stay is repriced using the current server-side pricing rules.
- Current dates remain unchanged until the request reaches COMPLETED.
- A request that reaches check-in before completion expires and cannot mutate the stay.
```

The initial implementation does not support moving an already-started stay. During an active stay, only `STAY_EXTENSION` is permitted.

## STAY_EXTENSION Contract

A `STAY_EXTENSION` is a specialized date change.

```text
requestedCheckInDate = originalCheckInDate
requestedCheckOutDate > originalCheckOutDate
```

Rules:

```text
- It may be recorded before check-in or during the active stay.
- The current Guatemala business date/time must still be before the current check-out boundary.
- Only nights after originalCheckOutDate are newly priced.
- The original confirmed stay amount is preserved.
- Availability, preparation buffers, and composed dependencies are recalculated for the extended effective range.
- If the existing stay has ended, the extension request is not eligible; a new reservation is required.
```

## Date Horizon and Request Lifetime

Accepted limits:

```text
Maximum requested check-out: 365 calendar days after the current America/Guatemala business date
PENDING_REVIEW lifetime: 24 hours from requestedAt
Positive-difference LifecycleRequestHold lifetime: 60 minutes from approval/hold creation
Public PENDING_PAYMENT reservation hold: remains 15 minutes
```

Expiration behavior:

```text
- An undecided PENDING_REVIEW request becomes EXPIRED after 24 hours.
- An expired request cannot be approved, repriced in place, or reactivated.
- A new request must take a new snapshot and calculate a new quote.
- A lifecycle hold that reaches expiresAt becomes EXPIRED and the lifecycle request becomes EXPIRED unless completion already committed.
- Pending/rejected/failed non-approved adjustment payment attempts remain historical and cannot apply dates after expiration.
```

## Pricing Contract

All prices are calculated server-side. Client-supplied totals, differences, nightly rates, fees, taxes, or discounts are never authoritative.

The current pricing implementation uses:

```text
subtotal = current baseNightlyPrice × nights
cleaningFee = 0
taxes = 0
discounts = 0
total = subtotal + cleaningFee + taxes - discounts
currency = USD
```

Any future nonzero cleaning fee, taxes, discounts, seasonal pricing, or one-time fee treatment requires an explicit documented rule before it may affect lifecycle repricing.

### Full date-change pricing

A `DATE_CHANGE` reprices the complete requested stay using current pricing:

```text
requestedTotal = current server quote for the entire requested date range
financialDifference = requestedTotal - originalTotal
```

The original confirmed amount remains immutable in the lifecycle snapshot even if the property price changes later.

### Stay-extension pricing

A `STAY_EXTENSION` preserves the confirmed original amount and prices only the added nights using current pricing:

```text
addedNightsSubtotal = current nightly price × nights from originalCheckOutDate to requestedCheckOutDate
requestedTotal = originalTotal + addedNightsSubtotal
financialDifference = addedNightsSubtotal
```

The initial extension calculation does not reprice original nights. It also does not duplicate one-time fees because current cleaning fee, taxes, and discounts are zero. Future nonzero fee behavior requires a separate decision.

## Financial-Difference Branches

### Positive difference

```text
financialDifference > 0
```

Accepted flow:

```text
Admin approves request
-> request becomes AWAITING_ADJUSTMENT_PAYMENT
-> create independent 60-minute ACTIVE LifecycleRequestHold
-> create linked PENDING Payment with purpose LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT
-> guest completes Tilopay checkout through a purpose-bound expiring handoff
-> Payment becomes APPROVED only after normal server-side provider validation
-> final serializable transaction revalidates request, hold, payment, reservation version, availability, buffers, and dependencies
-> apply requested dates and requested pricing
-> keep Reservation.status = CONFIRMED
-> request becomes COMPLETED
-> hold becomes RELEASED
```

Adjustment payment rules:

```text
- amount equals the exact positive financialDifference.
- currency equals the reservation currency.
- lifecycleRequestId is required.
- purpose is LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT.
- It must never be selected as the source for a standard, extraordinary, or unrelated refund unless the lifecycle compensation rules explicitly permit it.
- Multiple failed/rejected attempts may exist while the hold remains active.
- At most one approved adjustment amount may satisfy the request.
- Cumulative approved adjustment payments cannot exceed the required positive difference.
```

The payment handoff must be opaque, purpose-bound, request-bound, payment-bound, and expire no later than the lifecycle hold. It must not rely on an unauthenticated reservation-reference-plus-email lookup or expose a generic mutation endpoint.

### Zero difference

```text
financialDifference = 0
```

Accepted flow:

```text
Admin approves
-> one serializable transaction performs final availability/version validation
-> apply requested dates and requested pricing
-> keep Reservation.status = CONFIRMED
-> request becomes COMPLETED
```

No `Payment` and no `LifecycleRequestHold` are created.

### Negative difference

```text
financialDifference < 0
refundAmount = absolute(financialDifference)
```

Accepted flow:

```text
Before approval/completion, validate that the exact difference can be returned within the remaining captured INITIAL_RESERVATION Payment balance.
Admin approves
-> one serializable transaction performs final availability/version/refundable-balance validation
-> apply requested dates and requested pricing
-> keep Reservation.status = CONFIRMED
-> request becomes COMPLETED
-> create an exact PENDING lifecycle-adjustment Refund authorization linked to the request and initial Payment
-> execute and reconcile the refund through the accepted Phase 11.4 workflow
```

11.5.5 must add:

```prisma
RefundAuthorizationType.LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT
```

This authorization type is distinct from:

```text
STANDARD_POLICY — cancellation-policy refund
EXTRAORDINARY — discretionary compensation outside policy
LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT — exact negative price difference or compensation of an approved adjustment payment whose date transition cannot complete
```

Refund failure never restores the old dates after a completed operational change. The failed attempt remains auditable and releases its committed refundable balance according to the accepted refund rules.

If the full exact negative difference cannot be authorized within the remaining captured balance, the request cannot complete through the automatic negative-difference path. It must be rejected or handled through a separately approved exceptional settlement; the system must not silently reduce the amount owed to the guest.

## Approved Payment but Date Transition Cannot Complete

An approved adjustment payment does not by itself authorize a stale or unavailable date mutation.

Final completion must still fail safely when:

```text
- LifecycleRequestHold expired.
- Reservation.updatedAt no longer matches the accepted version.
- Requested dates became unavailable.
- A composed-listing dependency or preparation buffer now conflicts.
- Request or payment relation/status is invalid.
- The request was rejected, withdrawn, expired, failed, or already completed.
```

Required outcome:

```text
- Original reservation dates and price remain unchanged.
- Reservation.status remains CONFIRMED.
- Request becomes FAILED with a bounded reason code, unless it was already EXPIRED.
- Hold becomes RELEASED or EXPIRED with a bounded reason code.
- The approved LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT Payment remains financially APPROVED and auditable.
- An exact compensating RefundAuthorizationType.LIFECYCLE_ADJUSTMENT intent is created against that approved adjustment Payment.
- Refund execution/reconciliation is separate and cannot force the date mutation.
```

This prevents both lost money and unsafe date application.

## Availability Contract

Requested-date validation must use the existing availability domain with an explicit current-reservation exclusion.

```text
- Exclude only blockers produced by the current Reservation being changed.
- Exclusion includes that reservation's own effective stay and derived composed/preparation effects where applicable.
- Do not exclude another reservation, Airbnb event, manual block, maintenance block, preparation block, composed dependency, or active lifecycle hold.
- Overlap between old and requested dates is valid only for the portion owned by the current reservation and only when every unrelated blocker remains clear.
- Current reservation blockers remain active until completion commits.
- A positive-difference lifecycle hold adds requested-range blockers while the original reservation continues blocking its original range.
- Final completion revalidates the complete requested range inside a serializable transaction.
```

The hold must block:

```text
- Requested stay dates
- Requested preparation days before and after
- Every affected accommodation under the composed-listing dependency rules
```

The hold must not create unrelated `MANUAL_BLOCK` rows. `LifecycleRequestHold` remains the typed source of truth.

## Completion Mutation Contract

A successful completion updates the existing Reservation in one serializable transaction:

```text
checkInDate
checkOutDate
subtotal
cleaningFee
taxes
discounts
total
updatedAt
```

It preserves:

```text
Reservation.id
Reservation.status = CONFIRMED
confirmedAt
original lifecycle snapshots
all Payment/Refund/request/audit history
```

The request becomes `COMPLETED`, `completedAt` is set once, and any active lifecycle hold becomes `RELEASED` with a completion reason.

## Arrival Instructions and Phase 11.6 Boundary

A completed date change or extension must supersede pending/failed arrival-instruction intents tied to the old check-in snapshot. Existing `SENT` history is not rewritten.

The accepted Phase 10 scheduler/version guard may create or backfill a new arrival-instruction intent for the updated date when eligible.

The following lifecycle emails remain deferred to 11.6:

```text
RESERVATION_DATES_UPDATED
STAY_EXTENSION_CONFIRMED
```

11.5 must not send those messages early. Notification failure in 11.6 will never reverse a completed date change, extension, adjustment payment, or refund.

## Idempotency and Concurrency Contract

Every mutation must use:

```text
- Strict Zod input validation
- Client request UUID
- Permanent database idempotency key
- expected Reservation.updatedAt
- expected lifecycle request updatedAt/version
- expected Payment.updatedAt when payment is involved
- expected LifecycleRequestHold.updatedAt/version when a hold is involved
- Serializable transactions
- Bounded retry only for database serialization conflicts
```

Replay requirements:

```text
- Repeating request creation with the same clientRequestId returns the same lifecycle request.
- Repeating approval/rejection returns the accepted existing result without creating another hold or payment.
- Repeating completion never moves dates twice or duplicates a refund.
- An opposite decision after a final state is rejected.
- Concurrent approvals cannot create two ACTIVE holds or overfund the required difference.
- Concurrent completions cannot apply two mutations.
```
