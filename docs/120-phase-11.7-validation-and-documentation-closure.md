## Purpose

Close Phase 11 as one coherent cancellation, refund, authorized date-change, stay-extension, lifecycle-notification, and protected-history feature.

This subphase consolidates the accepted evidence from Phase 11.1 through 11.6, runs a reduced cross-phase regression, reconciles the authoritative documentation, and prepares the explicit handoff to Phase 12 Production Readiness.

It must not repeat every previously accepted matrix. It must verify that the accepted subphases still work together and that the final documentation accurately represents the implementation, operational boundaries, accepted heads, and deferred production work.

